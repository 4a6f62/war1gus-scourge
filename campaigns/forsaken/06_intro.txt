The outer towns of Stratholme stand before us, guarded by heavily armored Human Knights and Conjurers. To break their lines, our Necromancers have uncovered the dark rite of Unholy Armor at the Temple of the Damned.

Build up your Necropolis, besiege the human settlements with Meat Wagons and Death Knights, and claim the outskirts of Stratholme for the Scourge!
