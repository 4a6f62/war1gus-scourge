Deep within Plaguewood Bog, a rival warband challenges the supremacy of the Scourge. From the tormented spirits of the fallen, our Acolytes can now raise Spirit Towers to summon wailing Banshees.

Banshees hurl ethereal blasts and can call forth broods of venomous crypt spiders to overwhelm the living. Crush the enemy warband and expand the blight across Plaguewood!
